# Journey 01 — Work to Evening

Status: Hypothesis  
Version: 0.1  
Date: 2026-08-21  
Primary persona: [Persona A](../personas/PERSONA_A.md)

## User situation

The formal workday is ending, but the user's internal work mode continues. They may still be at a desk, in an office, commuting, or moving directly into errands, sport, relationships, or another appointment.

HALE does not judge the next activity. It helps the user notice the transition and choose how to enter their own time.

## Trigger hypotheses

- closing the laptop
- ending the last meeting
- starting the commute
- opening a distracting app automatically
- reaching a user-defined end-of-work time

## Invitation principle

Avoid blame such as “Are you doing this again?”

Prefer voluntary, concrete language:

- “Your workday is over. Would you like to arrive before your evening begins?”
- “What do you need for your evening: calm, clarity, or energy?”
- “Ten minutes before you continue?”

## Flow

1. Brief state check-in.
2. Choose desired direction.
3. Choose available time.
4. Receive an appropriate session with safety guidance.
5. Finish with an open choice, not a forced next action.

## Time

Primary entry hypothesis: 5–10 minutes, with 10 minutes as the initial default. Longer sessions remain available but are not framed as morally better or universally more effective.

## Directions

### Regulation and coherence

Default hypothesis for releasing work mode, strengthening bodily awareness, and creating space.

### Activation

Optional when the user wants to exercise or enter an active evening. No claims about oxygen saturation, blood cells, lactate clearance, mitochondria, recovery, or performance may ship without method-specific evidence and expert review.

## Ten-minute structure

1. Arrive without judgment.
2. Select an intention.
3. Follow a safe, suitable breathing practice.
4. Reconnect with body and surroundings.
5. Choose the next activity independently.

## Desired outcome

The user:

- notices body and needs more clearly,
- acts with less rush,
- enters the next conversation or task more intentionally,
- experiences partner time, friends, sport, errands, or rest as chosen life rather than another checklist.

## Audio, voice, and poetry

- Music is a central design element.
- Driving, high-quality electronic beats are an inspiration, not a final audio rule.
- “Mello Kind” requires exact artist identification and a reference link before research.
- All music requires a valid license.
- Voice should guide while leaving space.
- A ten-minute session may use one short original or rights-cleared impulse.
- Longer sessions may combine meditation, silence, poetry, and breathwork.

## Digital intervention

A normal iOS app cannot arbitrarily overlay its own pop-up on Instagram. A voluntarily authorized Screen Time integration may be explored using Apple Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings. This is a later research item, not an MVP dependency.

## Success criteria

- Users understand the recommended session within seconds.
- The experience begins and ends without pressure.
- Users report an appropriate state change.
- The ending supports autonomy rather than another engagement loop.

## Open questions

- Is 17:00 a useful default or should the anchor always be individual?
- Which five- and ten-minute techniques are safe and suitable?
- Should regulation be the default or should choices be neutral?
- Which audio direction fits the primary persona without excluding others?
- Which digital intervention is technically feasible and ethically aligned?
