# HALE Brand System

Status: Structure only. No final visual identity decisions have been made.

## Strategic tension

HALE should find a distinctive balance between:

- spiritual and grounded
- evidence-aware and warm
- premium and accessible
- artistic and intuitive
- calm and capable of activation

## Governance

A brand rule becomes binding only when:

1. its rationale is documented in Notion,
2. the decision is logged,
3. technical tokens or assets are committed here,
4. affected components and touchpoints are updated,
5. accessibility and rights checks pass.
