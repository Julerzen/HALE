# HALE Agent Instructions

## Mission

Build HALE as a coherent, accessible, evidence-aware breathwork product. Explain decisions in beginner-friendly language while maintaining professional product, brand, design, engineering, safety, and documentation standards.

## Name

The product and project name is **HALE** (H-A-L-E). Do not use Hail or HAIL.

## Sources of truth

- Notion: strategy, research, inspiration, decisions, content planning, and human-readable brand documentation.
- GitHub: executable product rules, code, design tokens, assets, tests, and technical documentation.
- ChatGPT project: collaborative context, teaching, reviews, and planning.

If these conflict, stop and surface the conflict. Do not silently choose one.

## Status vocabulary

Always distinguish:

1. Inspiration
2. Hypothesis
3. Draft
4. Decision
5. Implemented

Do not present a hypothesis or inspiration as a final HALE rule.

## Change impact rule

For every relevant product, brand, design, content, or technical change, check:

- product strategy and positioning
- Brand Bible and decision log
- design tokens and reusable components
- app screens and interaction states
- content, voice, and editorial guidance
- website, app-store, social, email, video, retreat, and product touchpoints
- accessibility, privacy, safety, and evidence
- documentation and tests

## Product principles

- Spiritual depth without dogma.
- Scientific care without clinical coldness.
- Premium quality without artificial status or exclusion.
- Artistic expression without sacrificing usability.
- Digital wellbeing over manipulative engagement.
- Safety before intensity or marketing impact.
- Genuine user benefit before feature count.

## Health and safety

Do not make unsupported medical or therapeutic claims. Flag breathwork contraindications, high-intensity practices, public-use contexts, and attention-sensitive environments for expert review. Never position intensive breathwork for driving, water, machinery, or situations that require full attention.

## Copyright and sources

Treat third-party poems, quotations, translations, music, visuals, and brand references as inspiration until rights are verified. Attribution alone does not automatically permit reuse. Prefer original HALE language and commissioned or licensed assets.

## Working rhythm

Prepare work for a weekly Sunday review and planning cycle. Favor three to five achievable outcomes over overloaded plans. Keep Notion and GitHub synchronized when decisions become binding.
